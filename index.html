01

Embedded systems · 2025

NeoPark

An Arduino-powered smart parking gate. RFID authenticates the vehicle, an infrared sensor confirms it's actually there, a servo drives the barrier and an LCD reports state — a complete access-control loop built from scratch.

How it works
  1. 1 An RFID reader scans the tag and checks it against the authorised IDs.
  2. 2 An infrared sensor confirms a vehicle is actually present at the gate.
  3. 3 Only with both true does the servo drive the barrier arm up.
  4. 4 The LCD reports the current state — reading, granted, denied, open.

Two independent conditions have to agree before anything moves, so a valid tag alone can't open an empty gate and a car alone can't force it.

  • Arduino
  • C++
  • RFID
  • IR sensing
  • Servo control
  • 16x2 LCD

Competitive robotics · 2025–26

VEX 19109M — UTS Circuit Breakers

A season-long build for the V5RC Push Back game: robot architecture, autonomous programming, driver control and the strategy work between matches. Iterated across the whole season against real competition data.

How the season ran
  1. 1 Design and build the drivetrain and scoring mechanisms for Push Back.
  2. 2 Program the autonomous routine and tune it against the real field.
  3. 3 Driver practice until the controls are muscle memory.
  4. 4 Scout opponents, set match strategy, then rebuild what didn't hold up.

The loop is the point — every competition returned data that changed the next revision of the robot.

  • VEX V5
  • C++
  • Autonomous
  • Mechanical design
  • Scouting
